## Skills and examples

- L121: "the value of practicing them": "them" grammatically points to
  "skills." Say "the etudes." Also "give yourself a self-test" is redundant;
  "test yourself" is enough.

- L129: Robertson was already introduced, so "what Robertson would call" is
  enough.

- L159: "combines various rolls, mixed... and backward" lists only two. Try
  "combines mixed (`p-m-i`) and backward (`a-m-i`) rolls."

- L182: "The larger rolls also use economy picking" overgeneralizes; say "Some
  of the larger rolls." There's also an unacknowledged tension: in the intro
  you call economy picking a crutch you overused, and here Giuliani prescribes
  it. One clause would turn that into a point, e.g. "so economy picking isn't
  banished, just put in its place."

- Hyphenation drifts: "down-beat" (L196), "down beats" (L217, L300),
  "off-beats" (L310). Standard is "downbeat(s)" and "offbeat(s)."

- L192 heading "Pinches and compound patterns": the body never says "compound
  patterns," but it does say "compound pinches" (L216), and that term isn't
  defined. Align the heading and the text.

- L216: "uses more compound pinches" has a double "more." Try "uses denser
  pinches."

- L231: "the fingers fill in between": between what? Probably "fill in above."

- L232: "familiar and foreign for" should be "foreign to."

- L231/L298/L322: number ranges use hyphens ("36-50"). En dashes are more
  correct, but that's optional. Just be consistent.

- L263 "Finger pairs" sits between the 36–50 and 51–65 groups and breaks
  the otherwise ascending order. That's minor, but Favorites is mostly finger
  pairs too, so it might fit better just before Favorites.

- L298: "is one of the more demanding" is missing a noun. Try "is one of the
  more demanding groups."

- L322 [main]: "the bass plays string 5, 4, 3, 4 repeatedly" is true only for
  the C bar. In etude 66 the G7 bar is 6, 4, 3, 4. Also, the string-3 note is
  played by `i`, not the thumb, per your own label (`pma p i p`). "Lower
  voice" covers that, but "running bass line" (L326) slightly misstates it.
  Suggest "the bass pattern (strings 5-4-3-4 under C, 6-4-3-4 under G7) stays
  fixed."

- L320 heading "Chord placement" vs. the body's "a pair of upper notes" / "an
  interval": the body describes double-stops, not chords. Either rename the
  heading ("Double-stop placement") or describe it as the pinch chord.

- L348: "Some of the later etudes combine several finger-pair techniques"
  leads into Favorites. Fine, but the reader might wonder why these aren't
  simply part of Finger pairs. Already decided, probably.
